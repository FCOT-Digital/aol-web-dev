# Project-Based Learning

* The website you want to make will be far more interesting to you than anything I could provide.
* Discovering the feature you want will be far more memorable than anything I could describe.
* Researching language features is a skill you will use in your exams and your career.

Therefore, the best way to learn is to make a project that you are interested in.
You will be more motivated to learn and you will remember what you learned better.
